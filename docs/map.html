Export

Replace the booths array in data/manual/booth_locations.json with this, then run epcot-fw manual to send it to the database.

Coordinates are WGS84 decimal degrees, six places. Pins dropped here are staged as location_precision: "mapped" — better than a pavilion-anchor stand-in, not as good as a GPS fix taken standing at the booth. A later survey always wins.
